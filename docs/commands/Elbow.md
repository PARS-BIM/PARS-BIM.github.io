# Elbow
With this command, you can Add elbow to the selected element with specified angle.


## Steps

First, select this command, and then choose the open end of a Pipe, duct, Cable tray or a conduit.

The directional guides will then appear at the free end of the element, making it easier to identify the direction of movement and adjustment.

To establish a reference point for the starting angle, keep in mind that the **blue line represents your 0° reference**.


<img src="https://pars-bim.github.io/docs/Assets/Elbow-Updated.png" alt="Elbow-Updated" width="500">

Additionally, by enabling the **Slope** option and specifying the **slope direction** and **slope percentage**, you can follow the same modeling pattern used previously for sloped elements.


To make modeling easier—especially when working with sanitary drainage piping—45-degree upward and downward directions are also available.

For faster modeling, a keyboard shortcut has been assigned to each direction. The following keys can be used:

Q, W, E, A, S, D, Z, X, C, R, F

As you may notice, all of these shortcuts are grouped together on the keyboard and arranged in a pattern similar to the positions of clock hands, making them easy to remember and use during modeling.
