# Spell check
Using this command, you can find spelling errors or naming inconsistencies.

## Steps
First, in the Common Tools panel, under the Sheets dropdown, click on Spell Check.

In the opened dialog, you can define the scope of your search within the project. For example, select Sheets, Views, Legends, or Schedules. You can also search through specific parameter values in the Custom Text Parameters to Check section.

Under Select Languages, choose the languages used in your project. If a language present in the project is not selected, you may encounter unnecessary error warnings.

<img src="https://pars-bim.github.io/docs/Assets/Spell-check.png" alt="Spell-check" width="500">

After setting the search scope and selecting the required language dictionaries, click the Start Scan button. Depending on the volume of text in your project, the scan process may take some time.

Once the scan is complete, a list of potential errors will be displayed. This list shows the error type, the location where the issue was found, and the flagged word itself. In the Suggestion/Fix column, it also recommends a likely correction.

You can accept the suggested word or manually type your own correction, then click Apply to save the changes.
